Approach

Whether you're at a demanding job, recovering from pain, or looking to build a stronger, healthier body, lasting strength begins with better alignment.

Each individualized session considers your body's history, how you recruit your muscles, and how you move through each exercise, not simply whether you can complete it. We work on alignment, strength, balance, and mobility to help you move with greater control and confidence.

By establishing proper mechanics, then adding greater challenge, you build a capable body and strength you carry into everything you do.

About Joan

Joan's approach to Pilates is rooted in a lifelong appreciation for movement. With a background in ballet and years of experience working alongside her chiropractor mother, she developed an early interest in anatomy, alignment, and the mechanics behind movement.

She is a 450-hour Pilates Comprehensive Certified Instructor and is continuing education in coursework with dance physiotherapists and physical therapists.

She brings this education into every session, helping clients understand their bodies and progressively develop strength with intention.
